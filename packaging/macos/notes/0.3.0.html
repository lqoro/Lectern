What's new in Lectern 0.3.0

  • A much faster speech model. On Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer, Lectern now offers Fastest (Parakeet), which runs on the Neural Engine. On an M4, two real livestream recordings (112 and 326 minutes) transcribed about 16–18× faster than with the model most of you have been using so far (Balanced, large-v3-turbo q5). The 112-minute one took 19 seconds instead of 5 minutes 23.
  • Accuracy is similar for English. Across those two recordings the words matched 95–97% of the time; most of the difference was foreign-language stretches where the old model made things up. Parakeet is the new recommended model on supported Macs.
  • Your current model keeps working. Lectern shows a banner offering the download (about 555 MB). Until it finishes, nothing changes, and you can say “Not now”. If Parakeet ever fails on a lecture, Lectern retries it with your previous model.
  • Intel Macs and macOS 12–13 keep using the Whisper models exactly as before.
  • Speeds are measured on an M4 Mac. Other chips will differ; M1 and M1 Pro numbers will follow.
