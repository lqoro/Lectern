What's new in Lectern 0.3.2

  • A redesigned reader. Serif reading text, a docked player at the bottom, a time gutter down the side, and a "Jump to current" button when you scroll away from the playhead.
  • A new Recording view. Start a recording and watch the live transcript fill in, with the elapsed time, status and storage in one calm screen.
  • A flatter library with a storage bar, so you can see how much space your lectures use.
