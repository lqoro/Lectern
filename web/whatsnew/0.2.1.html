What's new in Lectern 0.2.1

  • Updates inside the app. Lectern can now tell you when a newer version is out. It checks once a day, never installs anything on its own, and you can turn the check off in Settings ▸ Updates.
  • Model downloads work in the Mac app again (a certificate problem made them fail with “could not reach the download server”).
  • Opening Lectern always shows the current version, even if an older copy of Lectern was left running in the background.
