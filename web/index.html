        Before you record

        Three things worth knowing. It takes ten seconds.

        1. Recording laws differ by place In some states and countries, everyone being recorded has to agree. Elsewhere, one person's consent is enough.
        2. Your school may have its own rules Many classes need the instructor's OK, unless recording is an approved accommodation through disability services.
        3. Keep it for your own study Don't share recordings or slides without permission; they may be copyrighted. And double-check AI notes against the audio.
